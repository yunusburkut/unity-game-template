# Farming / life sim

Farm through the seasons and build a life in a small town; the core verb is the daily chore round.

**Camera (Unity):** a camera from above that follows the player over the farm and town, orthographic for 2D
(default); an angled perspective room view for a house you build and watch, as in The Sims.

## Defaults

- **bed-ends-day**: The clock runs through a day that starts and ends at home; going to bed ends it with a summary of
  what was earned and what is ready tomorrow, the game saves there, and staying up late costs little. Why: each day
  closes cleanly with a plan, and stopping is always safe. Break it when: time runs without days, a house watched
  around the clock. [harvest-moon-manual-1], [stardew-2]
- **crops-by-season**: Seeds are planted and watered, grow over a few days into a crop to pick and sell, and a season
  of set length changes what grows; some crops keep giving. Why: each day's chores build toward a harvest, and the
  season gives the plan an edge. Break it when: the farm is a garden for its own sake. [harvest-moon-manual-1]
- **one-neighbour**: A neighbour or two walk the town; talking or a gift fills a friendship meter, and they remember
  the last gift and talk about their own lives. Why: the town is people, not only fields. Break it when: a farm alone
  on purpose. [iwata-ac-2]

## Feel

- **field-shows-work**: Watered soil darkens at once, each crop shows a new stage every morning, and a ripe crop looks
  ripe. Why: the farm reads at a glance. Break it when: the farm is a ledger run from menus. [wada-1]
- **tool-on-tile**: The tile a tool will hit is marked before the swing, and holding the button works along a row.
  Why: chores are quick, and a stray click never wastes a seed. Break it when: free scattering is the charm.
- **harvest-pop**: A picked crop pops into the bag with a soft sound, and the night's sale counts up line by line.
  Why: days of care pay off in one moment. Break it when: harvests sell in bulk from a menu.
- **nothing-lost**: A missed watering makes a crop wait a day, never die, and the clock stops in menus and talks.
  Why: the player plans without fear and can stop at any time. Break it when: the harvest must feed you through a
  winter, a survival farm. [wada-1]

## Variants

- **cozy**: a long day, no energy to run out, nothing urgent.
- **planner**: a short day, energy that runs out, every hour a choice.
- **village**: a small garden, more neighbours, gifts that change what they say.

## Choices that shape the pitch

- **The life:** a farm you grow and sell from (default) · a house to furnish and neighbours to visit · a family
  whose needs you guide, as in The Sims.
- **The day's limit:** a day clock (default) · an energy bar as well · none, at your own pace.
- **Stakes:** none, a missed day only waits (default) · a target by a festival at the season's end · bills each
  night that a full day's work just covers.
- **View:** from above, following (default) · an angled room you build in.
- **Feeling:** the player feels ongoing planning by going to bed with tomorrow's harvest in mind, because each day
  closes cleanly and shows what is coming.

## Platforms

- PC: WASD moves, the mouse aims the tool tile, a held button works a row; number keys pick tools.
- Touch: tap a tile to walk there and use the tool, hold to work a row *(guess)*; a tool bar within thumb reach.
  Portrait or landscape both work; pick one in CLAUDE.md *(guess)*.
- Session: one in-game day per sitting fits a phone; the bed save is the natural stop. PC plays several days.

## Later

- Seasons and a calendar of festivals
- Townsfolk on daily schedules
- Needs and moods for every person, as in The Sims
- Friendships that grow into romance
- Fishing, mining and cooking on the side
- Barn animals to care for
- Machines that turn crops into goods
- Tool and house upgrades

## In a mashup

Farming leads the daily rhythm, the calendar and the save at night. Management leads the money when the farm is a
business. Fishing leads the cast and the catch, and the fish sell in the same bin. A dungeon or action half leads its
fights, and the day clock still runs. A creature collector leads catching, and the creatures help on the farm. A
visual novel leads the conversations.
