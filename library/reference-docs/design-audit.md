# Design audit

Ledger of the game measured against the design corpus (`docs/design-corpus/`). Cite corpus items only by their ID from `docs/design-corpus/README.md` (`P08`, `AG1`, `FP28`), and never restate a catalog row.
Every row cites a `file:line`, scene object or asset field that was re-checked in the tree. Line numbers come from the working copy on the audit date.
To use it: move the Status column along (`open` → `in progress` → `fixed <commit>` / `accepted`). A re-audit adds a new dated section, newest first. Findings the user keeps as they are move to *Accepted*.
Since 2026-09-23 every open row's Status also names its Linear issue (`MIX-NN`): Linear tracks the work, this file keeps the evidence. A row marked *merged* shares its issue with another row.
Bölüm 2 was deleted on 2026-09-25 (MIX-198, last state `3957db6`); its rows stay as evidence of what was measured, and their fixes no longer apply.

## 2026-09-22 — first audit against the corpus

Scope: `Assets/Mixtrol`, `Assets/Scenes`, `docs/`. Read-only; nothing was edited. Four audits
(mechanics, levels, feel, network) each went through a second verification pass.

**Priority:** P0 = blocks finishing a level, loses a required item for the session, or breaks a GDD
pillar or an AG rule · P1 = a feel, readability or attribution problem that weakens clips or the
feedback hierarchy · P2 = a content, replay or variety opportunity. **Size:** S = a config or scene
tweak, or under 30 lines · M = one component or solver change · L = a new system.

**Summary.** 80 verified findings were merged into 64 rows: **4 P0, 36 P1, 24 P2**, plus DA-65 added after the audit (decision D9). 28 of the original rows wait
on a design decision. Three themes stand out. **The objective can be lost:** a cauldron or box that
falls stays gone, and a checkpoint can bank it where it fell (DA-02, DA-03, DA-05, DA-08). Bölüm 1
could not be finished, because its geometry blocked the required cauldron from S1 onwards; DA-01 is
fixed (the cauldron stays in S1), pending a Play Mode check.
**Tier 1 is mostly unbuilt:** almost every pit kills. The one in-place save, the partner's air jump,
closes in about 0.4 s and online arrives a round trip late. R, today's only escape, restarts the
whole team at once (DA-07, DA-09, DA-10). **Blame lands on the wrong seat:** deaths the partner
caused name the victim, online cauldron stress blames the host for network delay, and remote bodies
fire full-screen effects on the local camera (DA-04, DA-11, DA-12, DA-24, DA-40).
